OUR BUSINESS MODEL

How Zafium Works

Zafium is an online technology and digital services business providing professional services to individuals, businesses, startups, and organizations worldwide.


Our services include web development, graphic design, animation and video production, artificial intelligence and machine learning solutions, data processing and analysis, web scraping and automation, research, and technical training.


Customers can review our available services through the Zafium website and submit a project request. For customized projects, the required scope, specifications, pricing, and estimated delivery time are agreed with the customer before work begins.


Once the customer selects a service and proceeds with an order, payment is collected through the payment methods available on the Zafium website.


After payment and confirmation of the project requirements, Zafium performs the agreed digital service. Completed work is delivered electronically through the Zafium website, email, cloud-based file delivery, or another mutually agreed digital method.


Zafium does not primarily sell physical products. Our business consists primarily of digital services, software-related solutions, creative work, data services, automation, and technical expertise.
